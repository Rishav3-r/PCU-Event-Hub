About PCU

PCET's Pimpri Chinchwad University (PCU), Pune is a state university established under the Govt. of Maharashtra Act No. V of 2023 and run by the Pimpri Chinchwad Education Trust (PCET).

Its 10-acre campus in the Pune Metropolitan Area brings together schools offering BA, BBA, BCA, B.Tech, B.Des, B.Sc, B.Pharm and MBA programmes under one roof.

The Campus Event Hub puts every cultural, technical, sports, academic and career event on a single page so students can discover it and register in seconds.

Contact: info@pcu.edu.in
